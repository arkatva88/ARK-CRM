Proposal & Overview

Scalevyn CRM Technical Specifications

v1.2.0-stable July 2026
Executive Proposal

Scalevyn CRM is a lightweight, custom-engineered client relationship and billing platform built for small-to-medium digital agencies. Designed from the ground up for streamlined invoice generation, quotation management, customer ticketing, and automated subscription tracking, it simplifies the daily financials of client operations.

Unlike bloated enterprise tools, Scalevyn is highly customized to support multiple business partners (Sajjad, Shrinidhi, Anish) sharing equal profits while utilizing their personal accounts for transactions. It tracks revenue in real-time and computes partner share balances dynamically based on the formula: (Total Revenue - Total Expenses) / 3.

Core Advantage: Absolute transparency in partnership finances. The platform keeps track of business revenue residing inside individual personal bank accounts, calculating who owes what to whom at any given point in time.

High-Level Architecture

Scalevyn CRM runs on a modular 3-tier architecture built with vanilla PHP. It uses a custom routing-prefix engine to automatically resolve directory path levels relative to the project root, allowing scripts in subfolders to seamlessly share global resources.

Relative Directory Prefixes (Dynamic Path Resolver)

Since Scalevyn CRM is structured hierarchically in directories, PHP scripts in subfolders (e.g. modules/billing/create_invoice.php) run at different nesting levels. The system utilizes a specialized header resolver to calculate path levels dynamically relative to the script filename.

includes/header.php
$project_root = str_replace('\\', '/', dirname(__DIR__)); $current_dir = str_replace('\\', '/', dirname($_SERVER['SCRIPT_FILENAME'])); $relative_path = str_replace($project_root, '', $current_dir); $levels = substr_count(trim($relative_path, '/'), '/'); if (trim($relative_path, '/') !== '') { $levels += 1; } $prefix = str_repeat('../', $levels); // Assets are then linked dynamic: echo "<link rel='stylesheet' href='{$prefix}assets/css/style.css'>";

This prevents hardcoded absolute URLs and enables seamless deployment across different local servers or production subdirectories without configuration modifications.

Interactive Operator Manual

Step-by-step procedures for the most common administrative tasks in the Scalevyn CRM platform. Click on a workflow header to expand details.

1 Setting up a Client Registry

Before issuing invoices or managing tickets, a client must exist in the database registry.

  1. Navigate to Clients in the sidebar.
  2. Click the Add New Client button in the upper right.
  3. Fill in the Client Name, Email addresses (two input slots are provided for primary and secondary communications), Phone, and Billing Address.
  4. Submit the form. The client is assigned a unique relational id.
2 The Sales Flow: Quotation to Invoice conversion

Scalevyn streamlines the sales pipeline by converting accepted estimates directly to dynamic invoices.

  1. Create Quotation: Navigate to Quotations -> Create New Quotation. Choose the client, input line items (quantity, unit price, description), and click save.
  2. Send & Accept: Print the quotation as a PDF, send it to the client. Once approved, update the status to Accepted.
  3. Convert to Invoice: Go to Invoices -> Create New Invoice. Select the client. An dynamic dropdown option "Convert from Accepted Quotation" will appear. Selecting the quotation auto-fills the entire line-items grid, client details, and total amounts.
  4. Set the Invoice Date, due date, and save.
3 Recording Receipts & Partner Balances

Payments received from clients must be logged into receipts to distribute shares and offset due invoices.

  1. Go to Receipts and click Create New Receipt.
  2. Select the Client. If they have outstanding invoices, choose the invoice number to link the payment.
  3. Enter the paid amount and payment date.
  4. Select "Credited To Account": This designates which partner's personal account received the money (e.g. Sajjad, Shrinidhi, or Anish).
  5. Save. The system automatically recalculates the Partner Bank Balances table on the main dashboard overview, reflecting this deposit in their ledger.
4 Managing Expenses & Withdrawals

Business operational costs (hosting renewals, templates, domains) paid out-of-pocket are reconciled as expenses, while partner payments are logged as withdrawals.

  • Expenses: Logged under Expenses. Designate the category, the partner who paid (debit from), amount, date, and optionally upload a PDF receipt. These subtract from both the business overall balance and that partner's personal account ledger.
  • Withdrawals: When a partner draws business profit, navigate to Withdrawals -> Log Withdrawal. Log the partner taking the money, the account it was drawn from (e.g., Sajjad, Shrinidhi, Anish, or "All"), date, and description. This reconciles profit balances equally.
5 Triggering Expiration Reminders (WhatsApp)

Scalevyn CRM identifies subscriptions expiring within the next 30 days and shows them in the Dashboard & Renewals pages.

  1. Navigate to Renewals. Expiring services are highlighted in yellow (expiring in 15 days) or red (already expired).
  2. Click the Reminder button in the service row.
  3. A new tab opens, launching WhatsApp Web (utilizing the wa.me click-to-chat API) pre-filled with a professional template:
    "Hello [Client Name], your subscription for [Service Name] is expiring on [Expiry Date]. Please renew to avoid service interruption. - Scalevyn.com"
  4. Click send to instantly text the customer.
